Agent Office — Developer ID signed & Apple notarized

Drag Agent Office.app to Applications, then open it from Applications.
To update, quit the existing app from its tray menu before replacing it.
Keep the same app location. Your existing profile and database are preserved.

Connecting another computer may ask for Local Network access.
Approve this only when you want to connect your offices. Signing identifies
the app; it does not grant privacy permissions on your behalf.

The app's release.json records its signing-time source and identity.
The DMG JSON records completed notarization; Apple tickets are stapled to
both the app and the DMG. Verify the supplied SHA256SUMS file.

Back up your profile before an upgrade that changes the database schema.
Restore the matching app and data backup if you need to roll back.
